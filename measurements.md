# Reference Paper (SentinelAI) Measurement Specifications

## Page & Layout Geometry
- **Page Size:** A4 (210 mm × 297 mm)
- **Top Margin:** 20 mm (56.7 pt)
- **Bottom Margin:** 20 mm (56.7 pt)
- **Left Margin:** 15 mm (42.5 pt)
- **Right Margin:** 15 mm (42.5 pt)
- **Column Count:** 2 columns (except Title, Author block, and Abstract on Page 1)
- **Column Width:** 86 mm (243.8 pt / ~3.38 in)
- **Column Gutter:** 8 mm (22.7 pt / ~0.31 in)

## Typography & Styling
- **Primary Font Family:** Times New Roman / TeX Gyre Termes (Serif)
- **Monospace Font:** Courier / TeX Gyre Cursor (for code/identifiers where applicable)
- **Title:** 18 pt, Bold, Centered across top of Page 1
- **Author Block:** 3 columns under title
  - **Author Name:** 11 pt, Bold, Centered
  - **Department & University:** 9.5 pt, Regular, Centered
  - **Email Address:** 9.5 pt, Regular, Centered
- **Abstract Heading:** 12 pt, Bold, Left-aligned above 2-column boundary
- **Abstract Body:** 9.5 pt, Justified, 1.15 line spacing
- **Keywords:** 9.5 pt, "Keywords—" in bold, followed by comma-separated terms
- **Section Headings (Level 1):** 11.5 pt, Bold, Numbered (`1 Introduction`, `2 Limitations of Existing Systems`, etc.)
- **Subsection Headings (Level 2):** 10.5 pt, Bold, Numbered (`3.1 Three-Tier Architecture`, etc.)
- **Body Text:** 10 pt, Regular, Justified, 1.15 line spacing, 3 pt paragraph spacing
- **Figure Captions:** 9 pt, Regular/Italic, e.g., "Figure 1: SemanticShield AI system architecture..."
- **Table Captions & Text:** 9 pt, Table title bold above table, table content 8.5 pt
- **Header (Pages 2+):** 9 pt, Italic, centered text above a 0.5 pt horizontal line rule.
  - *Header Text:* "SemanticShield AI — An Intelligent Agent for Phishing Simulation and Behavioral Threat Analysis"
  - *Page 1:* No running header.
- **Footer (Pages 2+):** Page number centered at bottom margin (9.5 pt, Regular).

## Figure & Table Sizing Rules
- **Figure Sizing:** Standard single-column width (~3.38 inches / 86 mm wide). Portrait or near-square aspect ratio (~1.2:1 to 1.4:1).
- **Text in Figures:** Minimum 6.5 pt readable serif/sans-serif font.
- **Table Style:** Clean academic tables with top and bottom horizontal borders, light grey header fill.
